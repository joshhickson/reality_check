# Reality Check: Your First Few Turns

Welcome to "Reality Check"! This guide will walk you through the first few turns of the game to help you understand the core mechanics and make your first session as smooth as possible.

## The Goal

Your goal in "Reality Check" isn't just to be the richest player. It's to build the most fulfilling life, however you choose to define that. You'll do this by managing four core stats: **Money**, **Mental Health**, **Virtue**, and **Sin**. The game ends when one player decides to end it by accumulating 20 **Narrative Momentum**, and the player with the best overall score wins.

## The Core Loop: Actions and Consequences

On your turn, you get **2 Action Points (AP)** to spend. Let's walk through a hypothetical first turn.

**Your Hand:** You start with a hand of cards. These represent opportunities, challenges, and choices.

**Your Turn (2 AP):**

1.  **Action 1 (1 AP): Play a Card**
    *   Let's say you have a **Virtue Card** that says "Volunteer at a local shelter." The effects might be `+2 Virtue` and `+1 Narrative Momentum`.
    *   You play the card, spend 1 AP, and adjust your stats accordingly. You've done a good deed, and your life story has moved forward a little.

2.  **Action 2 (1 AP): Draw a Card**
    *   You want more options for your next turn, so you spend your second AP to draw a new card from either the Virtue or Sin deck. Let's say you draw from the Sin deck this time, adding a little temptation to your hand.
    *   Drawing a card also gives you `+1 Narrative Momentum`.

**End of Your Turn:** You have spent your 2 AP. Your turn is over. You now have more Virtue and 2 Narrative Momentum points.

## Key Concepts Introduced

### Narrative Momentum (NM)

You gained NM from both of your actions. This is a crucial resource. It represents the "stuff" that happens in your life—the small stresses, minor accomplishments, and daily grind. You can spend 5 NM at any time on your turn to trigger a **"Life Happens"** event. These are major, story-defining moments that will present you with difficult choices and have a big impact on your game.

**The game ends when any player collects 20 NM!** This means you and the other players have direct control over the game's pacing.

### The Four Pillars

*   **Money:** Obvious, but important.
*   **Mental Health:** Crucial for avoiding **Burnout**. If your Mental Health drops to 3 or below, you'll be limited in what actions you can take until you recover.
*   **Virtue & Sin:** These measure your moral compass and have a significant impact on your final score.

### Social Capital

You'll notice some cards grant **Social Capital**. This is a spendable resource you can use for powerful social actions, like influencing a vote or calling in a favor. It's a way to wield power outside of just having money.

### Crossroads Events

At the start of your turn, a **Crossroads Card** is drawn. This card has a secret trigger condition (e.g., "if a player's Sin increases"). If you take an action that meets this condition, the event will interrupt your turn, forcing you to make another tough choice. This makes the game unpredictable and interactive.

## Your Next Turn

On your next turn, you'll have 2 more AP. Maybe you'll play that Sin card you drew? Or maybe you'll "Work Overtime" (a 2 AP action) to get a quick cash injection, at the cost of your Mental Health.

The core of "Reality Check" is balancing these decisions. Do you chase money at the cost of your sanity? Do you strive to be a saint, even if it leaves you broke? Your choices will shape your story. Good luck.
