# Game Design Proposal: Refining the "Death" System to "Burnout"

## 1. Executive Summary

The current "Death" system in "Reality Check" is a functional way to handle players who reach a critical state. However, the concept of "death" is grim and can feel overly punishing for a satirical party game. The "Purgatory Agent" mechanic is clever, but the journey to get there is a bit of a downer.

To better align this system with the game's tone and target audience, I propose rebranding "Death" to **Burnout**.

Burnout is a highly relatable concept for 21-35 year-olds. It's a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged or excessive stress. By reframing the penalty system around Burnout, we can create a mechanic that is not only more resonant but also funnier and more engaging.

## 2. The "Burnout" State

A player enters the Burnout state if:

*   Their **Mental Health** drops to 0.
*   They accumulate a certain number of **Shortcut** points (e.g., 20).
*   A specific card or event triggers it.

When a player enters Burnout, they are not eliminated from the game. Instead, they enter a "recovery" phase.

## 3. Burnout Mechanics

A player in the Burnout state experiences the following:

*   **Limited Actions:** On their turn, a player in Burnout does not roll to move. Instead, they can choose one of three "Recovery Actions":
    *   **"Go to Therapy":** Pay $500 to gain +1 Mental Health.
    *   **"Practice Mindfulness":** Do nothing for one turn to gain +0.5 Mental Health (rounded up).
    *   **"Blame Everyone Else":** Attempt to give another player -1 Mental Health. This requires a dice roll to succeed.
*   **Vulnerability:** Players in Burnout are more susceptible to negative events and can be targeted by certain cards.
*   **No Clout Gain:** A player in Burnout cannot gain Clout. In fact, they may lose Clout as they "go off the grid."
*   **Stat Decay:** While in Burnout, a player's Money and Clout will slowly decay, representing their inability to work or maintain their social standing.

## 4. The "Sabbatical"

Instead of becoming a "Purgatory Agent," a player in Burnout is on a **Sabbatical**. They are still in the game, but they are focused on recovery.

The Sabbatical has a clear goal: **raise your Mental Health to 5.**

Once a player's Mental Health reaches 5, they are no longer in Burnout. They can re-enter the game and resume normal play, but with some lingering effects:

*   **"Fresh Perspective":** For the first two turns after recovering from Burnout, a player is immune to Clout loss.
*   **"Financial Hit":** The player's Money will likely be much lower than when they entered Burnout.
*   **"Lessons Learned":** The player gains a "Burnout Survivor" trait, which gives them a permanent +1 resistance to Mental Health loss.

## 5. Why "Burnout" is Funnier and More Effective

*   **Relatability:** Everyone in the target audience has either experienced Burnout or knows someone who has. It's a shared struggle that is ripe for satire.
*   **Humor:** The recovery actions and the concept of a "Sabbatical" can be framed in a humorous way. The game can poke fun at corporate wellness trends and self-care fads.
*   **Engagement:** By keeping the player in the game and giving them a clear path to recovery, Burnout is more engaging than a simple "death" mechanic. The player has a mini-game to play while they recover.
*   **Strategic Depth:** The threat of Burnout adds a new layer of strategic consideration. Players will have to manage their Mental Health more carefully, and they might even try to push other players into Burnout.

## 6. New Card Examples

*   **"Your Boss Says 'We're a Family'" (Chaos Card):**
    *   **Description:** Your boss uses a classic corporate guilt-trip tactic.
    *   **Effect:** All players lose -1 Mental Health. Any player with Mental Health below 3 immediately enters Burnout.
*   **"An Influencer Posts About Their 'Digital Detox'" (Clout Card):**
    *   **Description:** A high-Clout player posts about their "struggle" with social media.
    *   **Effect:** If you are in Burnout, you can choose to "like" this post to gain +1 Mental Health, but the influencer gains +1 Clout.

## 7. Conclusion

Rebranding the "Death" system to "Burnout" is a small change that will have a huge impact on the tone and feel of "Reality Check." It transforms a grim and punishing mechanic into a relatable, humorous, and strategically interesting part of the game. By satirizing the very real phenomenon of modern burnout, this system will make "Reality Check" a more resonant and enjoyable experience for its target audience.
