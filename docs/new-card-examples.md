# Game Design Proposal: New, Funnier Card Examples

## 1. Introduction

This document provides a list of new card examples that incorporate the proposed design changes:

*   The **Clout System**
*   The rebranding of "Sin" and "Virtue" to **"Shortcut"** and **"Sacrifice"**
*   The **"Petty Cards"** category
*   The **"Burnout"** system

These examples are designed to showcase how these new systems can work together to create a funnier, more satirical, and more engaging gameplay experience.

## 2. New "Petty Card" Examples

*(Drawn at the start of each player's turn)*

**Card Title:** The Unnecessary Meeting

*   **Description:** You've been invited to a meeting that could have been an email.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** "Forget" to RSVP and don't show up. (+1 to your next dice roll, as you now have more time for actual work).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Attend the meeting and actively participate. (Lose your "Roll & Move" phase for this turn).

**Card Title:** The "Reply All" Apocalypse

*   **Description:** A company-wide email has been sent, and people are starting to "reply all" with "thanks!" and "got it!"
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Mute the thread and ignore the chaos. (No effect).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Reply all with "Please remove me from this thread." (Lose -1 Clout for being "that person").

**Card Title:** The Shared Fridge Standoff

*   **Description:** Someone has left a moldy, unlabeled container in the shared office fridge.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Quietly throw it away and tell no one. (You feel a sense of smug satisfaction. No in-game effect).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Send a company-wide email with a picture of the offending item. (Gain +1 Clout, but lose -1 relationship with a random player, who was the owner of the container).

## 3. New "Chaos Card" Examples

**Card Title:** Your Favorite Podcast Becomes Problematic

*   **Description:** The host of your favorite podcast has just been exposed for some questionable old tweets.
*   **Effect:** All players must choose:
    *   **Denounce the podcast:** Lose -1 Clout but gain +1 Mental Health.
    *   **Keep listening in secret:** Gain +1 Mental Health but lose -1 Clout if another player calls you out.

**Card Title:** A New Social Media App Is Released

*   **Description:** A new app called "Glimmer" has just launched, and everyone is joining.
*   **Effect:** All players must choose:
    *   **Join Glimmer:** Spend $100 on "early adopter" status. Start with +2 Clout on the new platform.
    *   **Ignore Glimmer:** Gain +1 Mental Health for avoiding the rat race, but start with -2 Clout if the app becomes popular later.

**Card Title:** Your Landlord Is Selling the Building

*   **Description:** You've just received a notice that your building is being sold to a private equity firm.
*   **Effect:** All players must roll a dice.
    *   **1-3:** Your rent is doubled. (Gain a "Rent" ongoing expense of -$500 per round).
    *   **4-6:** You are evicted. (Lose your next turn while you "find a new place to live"). Any player in Burnout is immune to this effect, as they are "already crashing on a friend's couch."

## 4. New "Shortcut" Card Examples (formerly "Sin")

**Card Title:** The "Quiet Quitting" Opportunity

*   **Description:** Your boss is on vacation, and no one is monitoring your work.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Do the absolute bare minimum. (Gain +2 Mental Health, but there is a 25% chance you get a "needs improvement" performance review next round, which will cost you money).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Keep working hard. (No effect).

**Card Title:** The Influencer Apology Video

*   **Description:** You've been caught in a minor scandal and need to do damage control.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Post a tearful, non-apology video. (Spend $200 on a professional lighting setup. Roll a dice: 1-3, you gain +2 Clout. 4-6, you lose -3 Clout for being cringe).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Take responsibility for your actions. (Lose -2 Clout, but gain +1 Mental Health).

## 5. New "Sacrifice" Card Examples (formerly "Virtue")

**Card Title:** Help a Friend Move

*   **Description:** A friend is moving to a new apartment this weekend and needs help.
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Spend your Saturday helping them. (Lose your next turn, but gain +1 relationship with that player and +1 Mental Health).
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Pretend you have other plans. (No effect).

**Card Title:** The Ethical Dilemma

*   **Description:** You discover that your company is using a supplier with questionable labor practices.
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Report it to HR. (There is a 50% chance you get a promotion for your integrity, and a 50% chance you get fired for "not being a team player." If you are fired, you immediately enter the Burnout state).
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Look the other way. (Gain +1 Shortcut point).

## 6. Conclusion

These examples demonstrate how the proposed systems can create a more dynamic, humorous, and satirical gameplay experience. The choices are more nuanced, the situations are more relatable, and the potential for emergent storytelling is significantly increased. By embracing the absurdity of modern life, these new cards will make "Reality Check" a truly memorable game.
