# GDD Revision Plan (Based on Master-Level Analysis)

## 1. Acknowledgment & Core Objective

The provided "Master-Level Review" is a high-quality, actionable document. The analysis correctly identifies the game's core strengths (thematic cohesion, social mechanics) and its most critical weaknesses (analysis paralysis, potential for feedback loops, underspecified math).

The objective of this revision phase is to integrate the report's key recommendations directly into the Game Design Document (GDD) to create a more balanced, engaging, and mechanically sound game.

## 2. Proposed Revision Plan

I will execute the following plan to update our design documents based on the report's findings.

### Step 1: Revise GDD - Core Systems & Balance

The first and most critical step is to address the mathematical and systemic imbalances. I will update `docs/game-design-document.md` with the following changes:

*   **Formalize the "Four Pillars" Economy:** I will add a new section explicitly stating the principle of "inefficient conversion" and adopting the proposed economic model to guide future card design and balancing.
*   **Refine the "Burnout" Mechanic:** I will add a "Circuit Breaker" rule to the Burnout mechanic, stating that a player cannot suffer from Burnout for one full round after recovering from it. This prevents the "death spiral."
*   **Balance "Community Impact":** I will replace the current placeholder for the "Builder" bonus with the recommended tiered system based on Virtue points to create diminishing returns.
*   **Refine Final Scoring:** I will update the end-game scoring section to use the proposed formula, including the non-linear bonus for high Mental Health.

### Step 2: Revise GDD - Player Experience & Flow

Next, I will address the mechanics that most directly impact the moment-to-moment player experience. I will update the GDD to:

*   **Add UI/UX Principles:** I will create a new section in the GDD titled "Information & UI/UX Principles" that explicitly calls for clear player aids, a minimalist HUD (for a digital version), and clear communication of game state to mitigate "Analysis Paralysis."
*   **Calibrate "Work Overtime":** I will add a designer's note to the Action Economy section to emphasize that the return on investment for "Work Overtime" must be carefully calibrated to be a fallback option, not a dominant strategy.

### Step 3: Update the Player Manual

Once the GDD has been fully revised with these new, more robust rules, I will update `docs/player-manual.md` to ensure it is consistent with the GDD. This will involve minor text changes to reflect the new mechanics and ensure the player-facing rules are clear and concise.

### Step 4: Submit for Final Review

After all documentation has been updated and aligned, I will submit the complete set of changes for your final review and approval.
