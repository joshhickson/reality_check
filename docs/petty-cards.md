# Game Design Proposal: "Petty Cards"

## 1. Executive Summary

The current card system in "Reality Check" focuses on major life events and significant moral dilemmas. While this is great for driving the narrative, it misses an opportunity to capture the humor of everyday modern life.

To make the game funnier and more relatable, I propose the introduction of a new card category: **Petty Cards**.

Petty Cards are low-impact, high-frequency cards that represent the small, absurd, and often passive-aggressive choices we make every day. They are designed to generate quick laughs and create a more textured, realistic, and humorous game world.

## 2. The "Petty Cards" Deck

**Deck Size:** 50-75 cards
**Frequency:** Players draw one Petty Card at the start of each of their turns, before rolling the dice.
**Impact:** Petty Cards have minimal impact on the four core stats, but they can provide small advantages or disadvantages, and they are a great source of flavor and humor.

## 3. Design Philosophy

*   **Relatability:** Petty Cards should be instantly recognizable to anyone who has ever worked in an office, lived with roommates, or used the internet.
*   **Low Stakes:** The choices on Petty Cards should not be life-altering. The humor comes from the fact that we often treat these small things *as if* they are major crises.
*   **Passive-Aggression:** Many Petty Cards should offer a choice between a direct, healthy action and a more passive-aggressive one.
*   **Flavor over Mechanics:** The primary purpose of Petty Cards is to add humor and personality to the game. The mechanical effects are secondary.

## 4. Card Examples

Here are a few examples of Petty Cards, designed to be used with the proposed "Shortcut" vs. "Sacrifice" naming convention.

**Card Title:** The Last of the Coffee

*   **Description:** You've just poured the last cup of coffee from the pot.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Quietly walk away. (+1 to your next roll, as you are now caffeinated and sneaky).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Brew a new pot. (Miss your next "Roll & Move" phase, but gain +1 relationship with the next player to take a coffee).

**Card Title:** Email typos

*   **Description:** You've just sent a very important email to a superior and you notice a typo.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Ignore it and hope they don't notice. (No effect).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Send a follow-up email correcting the typo. (Lose -1 Clout for being awkward).

**Card Title:** Grocery Store Encounter

*   **Description:** You see someone you know from a distance in the grocery store.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Pretend you didn't see them and hide in another aisle. (No effect).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Go over and make small talk. (Lose 5 minutes of your life that you'll never get back. No in-game effect, but you have to describe the boring conversation to the other players).

**Card Title:** The "We Need to Talk" Text

*   **Description:** Your partner sends you a text that just says "we need to talk."
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Put your phone on airplane mode. (Lose -1 Mental Health, but you can delay the conversation for one round).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Call them immediately. (Roll a dice: 1-3, it's a minor issue and you gain +1 Mental Health. 4-6, it's a major issue and you lose -2 Mental Health).

**Card Title:** The Group Chat In-Joke

*   **Description:** Someone in your group chat makes a joke you don't understand.
*   **Shortcut Option (✂️):** Just type "lol." (Gain +1 Clout for participating).
*   **Sacrifice Option (🙏):** Ask for an explanation. (Lose -1 Clout for being out of the loop).

## 5. Integration with Existing Systems

*   **Petty Cards and Stats:** The effects of Petty Cards should be minimal, but they can add up over time. A player who consistently chooses the "Shortcut" option on Petty Cards might find their core stats slowly eroding.
*   **Petty Cards and Clout:** Many Petty Cards will interact with the proposed Clout system, offering small gains or losses based on social media etiquette.
*   **Petty Cards and Relationships:** Some Petty Cards will have minor effects on relationships between players, creating small grudges or alliances.

## 6. Conclusion

Petty Cards are a low-cost, high-impact way to inject a significant amount of humor and personality into "Reality Check." They will make the game feel more like a living, breathing world, and they will create moments of laughter and recognition that will stick with players long after the game is over. By focusing on the small, absurd details of modern life, Petty Cards can make "Reality Check" a truly unique and hilarious experience.
