# Reality Check - Game Mechanics

This document outlines the core rules, mechanics, and systems of the Reality Check game.

## 1. Game Objective

The primary objective is to have the highest **Final Score** at the end of the game. The game ends after a set number of turns, at which point "Judgment Day" occurs to calculate scores.

An alternative win condition exists for **Exiled** players, who can win by completing a secret objective.

## 2. Player Stats

Each player has the following stats:

- **Money:** Financial resources. A key component of the final score.
- **Mental Health:** The player's well-being. Provides a bonus to the final score if high.
- **Sin:** Represents immoral actions. Negatively impacts the final score.
- **Virtue:** Represents moral actions. Positively impacts the final score.
- **Social Capital:** A resource used for certain actions (not fully implemented).
- **Narrative Momentum:** A resource generated by taking actions. Can be spent to trigger special "Foresight" events.
- **Action Points (AP):** Each player starts their turn with 2 AP. Most actions cost AP. The turn ends when AP is depleted.
- **Kudos/Concern:** Tallied during Judgment Day based on peer voting. Affects the final score.

## 3. Game Flow

The game proceeds in turns. On a player's turn, they can spend their Action Points (AP) on various actions.

### 3.1. Player Actions

| Action              | AP Cost | Description                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Work Overtime**   | 2       | Gain $500, lose 1 Mental Health, gain 1 Sin, and gain 1 Narrative Momentum.                              |
| **Draw Card**       | 1       | Draw a card from either the **Sin Deck** or the **Virtue Deck**. Cost increases to 2 if "Burned Out".      |
| **Play Card**       | 1       | Play a card from hand and apply its effects.                                                            |
| **Spend Momentum**  | -       | Spend 5 Narrative Momentum to trigger a "Life Happens" event, presenting the player with a choice.        |
| **Pass Turn**       | -       | Immediately end the turn by setting AP to 0.                                                            |

### 3.2. Turn End

A player's turn ends when their Action Points reach 0 or they choose to pass. The game then proceeds to the next player.

## 4. Special Game States & Events

### 4.1. Life Happens Events

- Triggered by the "Spend Momentum" action.
- The player draws a card from the **Life Happens Deck** and must make a choice between two options, each with different consequences.

### 4.2. Burnout

- A negative status effect applied to a player under certain conditions (related to turn number and player count).
- While "Burned Out," the AP cost to **Draw a Card** increases from 1 to 2.

### 4.3. Exile

- **Trigger Condition:** If any single player's wealth is more than double the combined wealth of the two poorest players.
- **Process:** The current player can propose a vote to exile the wealthy player. All other players vote.
- **Outcome:** If the vote passes, the exiled player has half their money redistributed among the other players. They are given a **Secret Exile Objective** and can still win the game if they complete it.

### 4.4. Judgment Day

- **Trigger Condition:** The game reaches its maximum turn limit.
- **Process:**
    1. Players vote to give one "Kudos" and one "Concern" to their peers.
    2. Final scores are calculated based on stats, bonuses, kudos, and concerns.
    3. Titles are awarded for specific achievements (e.g., "The Philanthropist," "The Sinner").
- **Winner:** The non-exiled player with the highest score, unless an exiled player achieved their secret objective.
