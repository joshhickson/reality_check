# GDD Deep Analysis & Comparative Review Prompt for Gemini 2.5 Pro

**Objective:** To perform a deep and comprehensive analysis of the provided Game Design Document (GDD) for the board game "Reality Check." The goal is to evaluate the game's design, identify its strengths and weaknesses, and compare its mechanics and themes to other successful games in the market.

**Context:** You are a senior game design analyst with expertise in modern tabletop games, social deduction games, and economic engines. You have been provided with a single document: the GDD for "Reality Check." You have no other information about the game, its developers, or its current state of implementation. Your analysis must be based *solely* on the information contained within this GDD.

**Core Task: Comparative Analysis**

Your primary task is to write a detailed report that addresses the following points:

1.  **Core Mechanical Loop Evaluation:**
    *   Analyze the central "Narrative Momentum" system. Is it an innovative and engaging core loop?
    *   Compare the dual-phase structure ("The Grind" vs. "The Turning Point") to the core loops of other successful games. Does it share similarities with games like *Scythe* (action selection), *Gloomhaven* (card-driven combat), or *The 7th Continent* (narrative exploration)?
    *   Evaluate the "inefficient conversion" principle of the game's economy. Is this a sound design choice for creating meaningful decisions?

2.  **Thematic Resonance & Market Positioning:**
    *   Analyze the game's central theme of "satirical modern adult life." How well do the mechanics (Burnout, Social Capital, Exile Protocol) support this theme?
    *   Compare "Reality Check" to other thematically similar games, such as *The Game of Life*, *Monopoly*, or more modern satirical games like *Cards Against Humanity* or *Secret Hitler*.
    *   Based on its theme and mechanics, what is the likely target audience for this game? Where would it sit on a shelf in a board game store?

3.  **Advanced Systems & Player Interaction:**
    *   Evaluate the "Exile Protocol." Is it a strong catch-up mechanic? Compare it to the catch-up mechanics or player elimination alternatives in other competitive games like *Settlers of Catan* or *Twilight Imperium*.
    *   Analyze the "Social Capital" and "Community Impact" systems. Do these systems create a compelling social deduction or political layer to the game? How does this compare to the social mechanics in games like *The Resistance*, *Dead of Winter*, or *King of Tokyo*?
    *   Evaluate the "Crossroads" interrupt system. Is it likely to be a fun, interactive mechanic or a frustrating, random one?

4.  **Strategic Depth & Player Archetypes:**
    *   Review the defined player archetypes ("The Hustler," "The Zen," "The Agitator," "The Puppeteer," "The Community Pillar"). Do these archetypes seem viable and distinct?
    *   Based on the rules, which archetype do you predict would be the most and least powerful, and why?
    *   Are there any obvious dominant strategies or potential exploits that emerge from a close reading of the rules?

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as a formal analysis report. Use clear headings, bullet points, and specific examples from the GDD to support your claims. The report should be insightful, critical, and constructive, providing actionable feedback for the game's designers.

**Final Deliverable:** A comprehensive report that evaluates "Reality Check" and provides a detailed comparison to other successful games, highlighting its unique strengths and potential weaknesses. You have no further information and cannot ask clarifying questions. Your analysis must be based entirely on the GDD provided.
